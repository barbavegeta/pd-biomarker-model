    Parkinson's disease biomarker model

    Parkinson's disease module of the clinical biomarker core. It narrows diagnoses to parkinsonian disorders, binds clinical assessments to MDS-UPDRS, MoCA and Hoehn and Yahr, and adds instrument-specific validation rules.

    URI: https://w3id.org/pd-biomarker-model

    Name: pd_biomarker_model
